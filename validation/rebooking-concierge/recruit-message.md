# Recruitment message

Two variants exist because the audience isn't uniform: Shrf's Dutch-speaking
personal/referral network needs different framing than English-speaking
waitlist signups and cold Facebook/LinkedIn strangers. **Neither variant
claims a prior conversation that didn't happen**, that was the bug in the
previous version of this file (the Dutch opener said *"Uit onze eerdere
gesprekken bleef één ding hangen,"* which is false for every candidate on
the current shortlist, cold or warm). Fixed 2026-08-16.

Send personally, one at a time. Photographers and videographers first.
Never mention Freelens, AI, or agents, the experiment is the service, not
the software; naming software changes what they think they're judging.

## Which variant goes where

| Tier | Who | Variant | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Shrf's personal network (warm) | A (Dutch) | Adjust the opening line to the real relationship if you want, reference something true and specific, just not an "earlier conversation" that didn't happen. |
| 4–6 | Site waitlist (opted-in strangers) | B (English), **[Waitlist opening]** | Honest to reference their signup, but don't say "FrontDesk", the one confirmed record (2026-08-06) predates FrontDesk and has no `product` field, so it opted into something else, probably the old calculator. Say "the site," not the product name. |
| 7–8 | Referrals from slots 1–6 | A or B, matched to the referred person's language | Comes with a warm intro from whoever referred them, use that real intro as the opening line, not either bracketed default. |
| 9 | Cold: Dutch ZZP Facebook groups | A (Dutch), generic opening exactly as written | **Do not add any relationship claim.** 15-minute trust call before the intake ask. See README.md. |
| 10 | Cold: LinkedIn + International Freelancers in Nederland | B (English), **[Cold/warm opening]** bracket | Do not use the waitlist bracket, they didn't sign up for anything. 15-minute trust call before the intake ask. See README.md. |

## Variant A (Dutch)

Premise: no claimed "earlier conversations." Client list used for nothing
else and deleted afterwards.

---

Hoi {naam},

Mag ik twee weken iets voor je doen, gratis, als experiment?

Iets wat ik van veel creatieve freelancers hoor: nieuwe klanten vinden is
het echte werk, en oude klanten opnieuw benaderen voelt ongemakkelijk,
dus het gebeurt niet.

Het experiment: jij stuurt me een lijstje van tien oude klanten (naam, wat
je voor ze deed, wanneer, ongeveer welk bedrag) en twee mails die je ooit
zelf aan een klant schreef, zodat ik jouw toon ken. Ik schrijf dan drie
korte mails aan de drie klanten waar op dit moment een échte aanleiding
voor is, geen "even checken hoe het gaat", maar een concrete reden
waarom nú het moment is.

Jij past ze aan zoals je wilt en verstuurt ze vanuit je eigen mail. Wat er
terugkomt is van jou. Ik wil alleen weten óf er iets terugkomt.

Waarom ik dit doe: ik test of dit als dienst moet bestaan. Jouw lijstje
gebruik ik nergens anders voor en gooi ik daarna weg.

Doe je mee?

{jouw naam}

## Variant B (English)

For waitlist recruits the opening honestly references their signup, we
can't pretend not to know them. For every other tier using this variant,
use the cold/warm bracket instead; it doesn't claim any relationship.
Framed as a manual service, not a product demo, so they judge the service,
not the software, a deliberate, minimal deviation from the "never name
the product" rule for the waitlist case specifically, flagged at sign-off.

---

Hi {name},

[Waitlist opening, tier 4–6 only: You signed up on the site a while back,
before anything launches, I'm running the whole thing by hand for five
people, free, as an experiment. Can I do it for you?]
[Cold/warm opening, tiers 7–8, 10: Can I do something for you, free, for
two weeks, as an experiment?]

Here's what I keep hearing from creative freelancers: finding new clients
is the real work, and reaching back out to past clients feels awkward, so
it doesn't happen.

The experiment: you send me a list of ten past clients (name, what you did
for them, when, roughly the budget) and two emails you once wrote to a
client, so I know how you sound. I'll write three short emails to the
three clients where there's a genuine reason to get in touch right now,
not "just checking in," but a concrete reason why now is the moment.

You edit them however you like and send them from your own email.
Whatever comes back is yours. I only want to know whether something comes
back.

Why I'm doing this: I'm testing whether this should exist as a service.
Your list won't be used for anything else and gets deleted when we're done.

In?

{your name}

---

**Notes for you:**
- Do not mention Freelens, AI, or agents in either variant.
- If someone hesitates on sharing client names: first names + company type
  is enough ("Lisa, marketingmanager bij een meubelmerk" / "Lisa, marketing
  manager at a furniture brand").
- Book the 10-minute intake call in the same thread if the list doesn't
  arrive within two days, the list is the first drop-off point.
- Cold tiers (9–10) get a 15-minute trust call before the intake ask, see
  README.md.
