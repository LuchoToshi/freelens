Generated by `lib/tax/oldVsNew.test.ts` from the live engine. Do not edit by hand; it is rewritten every test run.

All figures are for the 2026 tax year, a taxpayer under AOW age, config `nl-2026.1`.

## The two old models

**Old A, the per-payment rule.** 30% of revenue excluding VAT. This is the number the tool put in front of most people. It taxes revenue, so it punishes anyone with costs.

**Old B, the guided estimate.** 30% of profit, plus Zvw as a separate line. Better, but still flat where Box 1 is progressive, and blind to the zelfstandigenaftrek, the MKB-winstvrijstelling and both heffingskortingen.

**New.** The engine: real brackets, real deductions, real credits, Zvw with its own capped base.

## Case 8, with the loonheffing filled in

Case 8 above assumes nothing was entered for tax the employer already withheld, which is the cautious reading. Enter a realistic € 2.250,00 of loonheffing on the € 30.000,00 salary and the figure drops from € 10.078,67 to € 7.828,67.

The Zvw part is unchanged at € 796,01, because Zvw here is charged on business profit only. Only the income tax moves.

Either way the old per-payment rule under-reserves this person, because it only ever sees the € 20.000,00 of freelance revenue and never the salary that pushes that profit into a higher bracket. Against the corrected figure it is still under by € 1.828,67.

## The headline

The per-payment rule is wrong in both directions. Its worst over-reserve is case 9 (High-cost, €80.000 revenue minus €35.000 costs), over by € 16.720,20. Its worst under-reserve is case 7 (Established, €95.000 profit), under by € 4.914,90.

Over-reserving is not harmless. Money parked against a bill that will never arrive is money not paid to yourself, and for a freelancer with irregular income that is the difference between a lean month and a fine one. Under-reserving is worse: it is a bill you cannot pay.

The single largest driver of the difference at low and middle incomes is the heffingskortingen. The old model ignored them entirely. On case 1 they cancel the income tax outright, leaving only Zvw, which the flat model had folded into one number and hidden.
